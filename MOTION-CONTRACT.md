# Cinematic motion contract

## Reference role

`D:\Desktop\website\耳机网站素材2\黑色耳机.mp4` is used only as evidence for camera rhythm and transition structure. Its product, styling details, text, and audio are not copied into this project.

## Frozen decisions

1. Cover journey: the camera starts on the lower body of the complete real Thinline, travels along the neck, and settles at the headstock before the visitor enters.
2. Memory moment: the camera performs three measured focus pulls across the real neck pickup, bridge assembly, and control area before pulling back to the headstock.
3. Carrier: Canvas 2D transforms of the official product-number-matched photographs; no generated guitar frames, inferred 3D model, or synthetic internal view.
4. Invariants: guitar pixels, component shapes, screw positions, pickup layout, bridge, pickguard, controls, F-hole, headstock, logo, strings, and tuning machines remain sourced from part number `0374067521` photographs.
5. Acceptance: no inner rectangular crop edges at 1440x900, 1280x720, 768x900, 390x844, or 390x720; transitions remain reversible; Reduced Motion uses short crossfades; no runtime network dependency.

## Allowed motion treatment

- Rotate, translate, scale, opacity, brightness, contrast, blur, edge feathering, and soft light over the original pixels.
- Focus rings and light may identify visible exterior components without moving or redrawing their pixels.
- The cover camera may travel across a rotated full-product master because rotation does not alter the instrument structure.
- Missing rear surfaces, hidden cavities, wiring, fasteners, pickup undersides, and other unseen geometry must not be generated or illustrated.
- A true exploded view remains out of scope until isolated, rights-cleared photographs or a verified model of the actual parts are available.

Independent reusable effect implementation; no copied proprietary source or assets.
